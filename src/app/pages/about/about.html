About Elohim Computers

Technology you can trust, from the people who set it up and keep it running.

Our story

Elohim Computers sells and supports computers, printers, networking equipment and software for homes, schools and businesses. We started with a simple idea: people should get honest advice and reliable equipment, and help when something goes wrong.

Today we combine a growing shop with hands-on technical services, so you can buy, set up and maintain your technology in one place.

Our mission

To make dependable technology accessible and to support every customer with honest advice and skilled service.

Our vision

To be the most trusted technology partner for individuals and businesses in our community.
